I am a Research Fellow at Duke-NUS Medical School, working at the intersection of statistics and machine learning. My research focuses on developing principled methodologies for robust and generalizable learning from heterogeneous, multi-source data, addressing fundamental challenges such as distribution shift, missing information, and privacy constraints.

My research spans distributionally robust learning, transfer learning, federated learning and inference, Shapley value-based interpretability, and algorithmic fairness, with an emphasis on methodological innovation and broad applicability.
