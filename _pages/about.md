I am a Research Fellow at the [Digital Medicine Lab](https://blog.nus.edu.sg/liunan/). My research develops statistical and machine-learning methodology for trustworthy learning and decision-making from heterogeneous, distributed, incomplete, and privacy-sensitive data. 

Major areas include transfer learning, federated learning and inference, Shapley value-based interpretability, missing data, and algorithmic fairness, with applications to healthcare and other high-stakes real-world systems.
